Services

Nutrition Counseling

We work with individuals ages 18 and older. We occasionally work with adolescents — please reach out to discuss.

We offer nutrition counseling for:

  • Eating disorder recovery (Anorexia Nervosa, Bulimia Nervosa, Binge Eating Disorder, OSFED, ARFID)
  • Disordered eating or chronic dieting
  • Food or body image concerns
  • Intuitive eating
  • Intuitive movement and nutrition guidance for active people
  • Management of chronic health conditions (gastrointestinal conditions, digestive issues, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, and diabetes)
