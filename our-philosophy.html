Our Philosophy

Our counseling approach is guided by the following frameworks

All-Foods-Fit

We believe all foods benefit our bodies in various ways! These labels can detrimentally impact how we perceive food and ourselves due to the morality attached to them. We encourage embracing nuance to support a more peaceful relationship with food.

Anti-Diet

Diet culture is a racist, classist, and sizeist system of beliefs that attaches morality to thinness, the pursuit of weight loss, and certain ways of eating. We work with clients to move away from diet mentality and toward greater freedom with food.

Weight Inclusivity

We are committed to providing weight-inclusive, fat positive nutritional counseling, as we believe that people in all bodies are deserving of respect, acceptance, and ethical, equitable care. Our practice is aligned with Health at Every Size® principles.

Anti-Oppression

We are committed to practicing from an anti-oppressive lens, meaning we will: 1) continually identify and challenge our biases, 2) name ways in which diet culture upholds oppressive systems, and 3) take action within our practice and in our communities.

Intuitive Eating

The Intuitive Eating framework is based on 10 principles and involves recognizing and responding to your body's cues through an interplay of emotion, instinct, and thought. This framework supports restoring trust with your body.

Intuitive Movement

We hold the belief that movement is most supportive and beneficial for our health and well-being when it comes from a place of joy, connection to the body, and self-care, rather than obligation.
